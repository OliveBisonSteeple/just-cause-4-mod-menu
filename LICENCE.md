## 🧩 About

**Just Cause 4 helpers** is a curated list of Just Cause 4 open-source tools:

- **Overlays** — on-screen helpers, layout notes, HUD tweaks, map markers
- **Quality-of-Life Helpers** — vehicle spawner, weapon manager, tether mods
- **Research Notes** — physics layouts and config notes for educational use

Based on publicly available community tools and overlay helpers.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Gameplay Helpers
- Vehicle Spawner — spawn any vehicle on demand
- Weapon Loadout — quick switch and infinite ammo notes
- Tether Mods — enhanced grappling and tether options
- Chaos Objects — highlight and interact with destructible targets

### 🗺️ Map & Navigation
- Full Map Reveal — unlock all regions and points of interest
- Waypoint Manager — set and teleport to custom waypoints
- Supply Drop Marker — call in drops anywhere

### 🧰 Quality-of-Life
- Slow Motion — adjust game speed for study
- No Fall Damage — toggle fall protection
- Fast Travel Anywhere — instant travel from map
- Weather Control — change weather conditions
